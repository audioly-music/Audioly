Install Audioly

On Android, download the APK, open it, and allow installation when Android asks. On desktop, choose the installer for your operating system. For macOS, select Apple Silicon or Intel to match your Mac.

Moving from Audioly Dev? Back up your library in the old app, install Audioly, then restore your backup.
